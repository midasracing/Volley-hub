# VOLLEY HUB — SIDEOUT / Rive identity exploration

## Premise
Identity first. Motion second. The VOLLEY HUB wordmark is hand-constructed from native vector shapes in `scene.rml`, not simulated by CSS. It must hold up when still.

## Typographic proposal
- Remove the ordinary rounded-square 'V tile', a generic app aesthetic.
- A distinctive **circular O** provides a physical contrast against nine mostly rectilinear custom glyphs.
- Narrow stroke terminals and disciplined tracking; soft white VOLLEY and restrained cold-blue HUB.
- Body size 234×48 — the mark must remain readable on small phones.

## Motion grammar
Three natural beats inspired by a volleyball rally (receive / set / attack):
1. The V anchors the rhythm, with the remaining VOLLEY letterforms joining almost as one.
2. The O briefly flies as the ball on a compressed arc and locks into its counter.
3. HUB arrives decisively, followed by one restrained light sweep. Then everything is still.

No permanent RGB glitch, particles or neon fog. Rive state machine controls the one-shot scene. Touch can replay; system reduced motion keeps static semantic lettering.
